Argos direct English/Turkish model attribution

The included direct language packages are the unmodified inference members of
the official translate-en_tr-1_5 and translate-tr_en-1_5 archives. Their original
README files and corpus citations are preserved in the model directories.

The upstream maintainer states that Argos model-training work is covered by
MIT/CC0 in the public model-license discussion:
https://github.com/argosopentech/argos-translate/issues/76#issuecomment-815704991

The original Argos project MIT copyright and permission notice is included
separately. This attribution does not grant new rights in the cited corpora,
Wiktionary or other training material. No training corpus is redistributed.
Unused Stanza tokenizer weights are excluded; inference weights are unchanged.
This notice is not a legal-review certificate or a claim of dataset ownership.
